Tideline

A calm, typographic forecast of tides and moon phases for UK coasts — charted data as a daily rhythm, not a dashboard.

Tideline displays tide tables the way people actually use them: “the beach is walkable between 14:40 and 19:10.” No scrolling charts full of numbers.

It aggregates UK tide predictions with moon phase and a friendly plain-language summary, wrapped in a Svelte front-end and a Rust API that caches predictions for a month at a time.
