The alarm went off at five. By six the shore was invisible and the water had
turned the colour of unbleached paper. I waited an hour for the rowboats to
drift into frame, shooting maybe six frames in total.

> Fog is patient. It rewards the same.

The trick with mist is to underexpose slightly — otherwise the camera tries to
make everything a cheerful middle grey and the whole mood collapses.
