A parking structure near the harbour, photographed on an evening walk. The
building's architect reportedly hated the planting plan; the plants did not
care what the architect thought.

I keep returning to places where maintenance has quietly stopped — not
dereliction exactly, just the beginning of a negotiation between concrete
and green.
