**Paper Static** is a monthly dispatch about generative art that anyone can
rebuild. Each issue takes one technique — dot grids, birdsong as sound art,
subpixel geometry — and explains it down to the maths, with copy-paste code.

The whole thing runs on Hugo and a static RSS feed. No analytics, no images
with tracking, just ink and interference.
